## 🛰️ 1. Dynamic Decryption Protocol: The 3-Step Process

The updated system utilizes a **Procedural Decoding Pipeline**. Instead of a direct solution, each signal requires **3 functional steps** to achieve a full override.

1.  **Step 1: Signal Intercept**
    - Click a functional button.
    - Result: The Log will confirm "Preliminary Data Recovered." 
    - Note: This provides a hint or a partial glimpse of the decrypted text.
2.  **Step 2: Pattern Sync**
    - Click another functional tool.
    - Result: Confirms "Signal Pattern Recognized." 
    - Note: This provides a refined 66% preview and often populates the buffer with a working draft.
3.  **Step 3: Neural Bridge (Auto-Fill)**
    - Click a third functional tool.
    - Result: "DECRYPTION SEQUENCE FINALIZED."
    - **Outcome**: The system **automatically types the final solution** into your input box. Press **EXECUTE DECRYPT** to finalize.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For every mission, only **3 out of 5 buttons** are functional. Noisy buttons will deduct **10 seconds** from your mission clock!

## 🛠️ 3. Toolset Tutorial

-   **FREQ_ANALYSIS**: Best for Initiate-level Caesar shifts. It identifies the most common character and suggested the shift offset.
-   **BASE64**: Decodes standard Base64 encoding. Essential for missions that end with `=`.
-   **BINARY**: Converts bit-streams into text. Look for groups of 8-bit characters.
-   **XOR**: High-level bitwise decryption using a secure 128-bit protocol.
-   **CAESAR**: Allows you to cycle through all 26 shift combinations.
-   **ROT13**: A specialized Shift-13 Caesar tool, frequently used in Omega protocols.
-   **PROGRESSIVE**: Handles shifting logic where the offset increases for every character.
-   **HEX**: Decodes hexadecimal data strings into readable text.
